About

I'm Aagaman. I'm an engineering student from Nepal, and I'm obsessed with understanding how things actually work.

I don't like stopping at the surface. If something interests me, I want to take it apart, understand the pieces, see how they interact, and eventually build something myself. That's what pulled me toward software, AI, and systems. I also spent a lot of time exploring trading. It became another way for me to study systems — probability, risk, feedback, and decision-making under uncertainty. I'm still early. Most of what I'm building is unfinished, and a lot of what I'm learning will probably change the way I think again. That's fine. I'm here to build, understand, and improve.
